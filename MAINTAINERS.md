# Maintainers

Maintainers are individuals, appointed by the steward (`theyexist`). The steward
role is held by a person on the organisation's behalf.

| maintainer | role | since |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [@DamonOehlman](https://github.com/DamonOehlman) | steward | 2026-11-19 |

A new maintainer is added by a pull request that adds a row here. A maintainer
may merge an authored row that meets the evidence standard, and may accept or
withdraw one under the dispute process.
